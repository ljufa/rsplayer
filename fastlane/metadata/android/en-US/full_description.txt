RSPlayer is an open-source music player written in Rust, with a bit-perfect playback engine and no cloud accounts or tracking.

<ul>
<li>Plays FLAC, MP3, ALAC/M4A, OGG Vorbis, WAV and AIFF from your Music folder</li>
<li>Library browsing of your Music folder, playlists and synchronized lyrics</li>
<li>Internet radio and a podcast client that resumes episodes where you stopped</li>
<li>Parametric EQ, loudness normalization and automatic resampling</li>
<li>Multiroom: synchronized playback across RSPlayer devices on your network</li>
<li>Media notification and lock screen controls, background playback</li>
</ul>

The app runs the player engine on the device itself. The same engine can also run as a headless server on a NAS or Raspberry Pi.

Music access is limited to audio files in shared storage (permission to read audio and images). Optional battery optimization exemption keeps playback alive with the screen off.
