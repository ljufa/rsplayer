# Privacy Policy

The privacy policy has moved to **[rsplayer.de/privacy.html](https://rsplayer.de/privacy.html)**.

## Optional scrobbling services

Scrobbling is off by default. Only when you turn it on in **Settings, Scrobbling**, RSPlayer sends to the service you chose ([ListenBrainz](https://metabrainz.org/privacy) or the server URL you entered, and/or [Last.fm](https://www.last.fm/legal/privacy)), for each track you play from your library: artist, title, album, album artist, track number, duration, MusicBrainz ids if your files carry them, and the time you started listening. Your token, API key, secret and session key are stored on your RSPlayer device and are only sent to that service. Radio and podcasts are never reported.
